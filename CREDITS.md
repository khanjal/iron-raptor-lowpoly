## Used as references only (nothing copied)
The detail and triangle counts of the models were compared with free CC0 packs: the Kenney Car Kit and Quaternius's Farm Animals and Animals Pack (listed so the comparison can be checked). No file, mesh or texture from them is in this repository.

## Tools
Models are built with Blender (https://www.blender.org) through Python scripts. The output of a Blender script is not covered by Blender's GPL.
