# Alien tech (brief)

Scope: the carriable ship parts, hand devices and crash wreckage (the landed ship's interior and terminals came with the ships PR), as the game's prop catalogue lists them (category "Alien ship and alien tech", `PC-SH-727..763`). They are fictional, so there is no real-world size; the sizes are the game's.

- **Design language** (`ships.py`, `ship_props.py`): pale `alien_grey` hull, dark `metal_dark` trim, hexagonal sections, `glow_cyan` wherever there is energy (`glow_green` / `glow_amber` / `alien_violet` for status and crystals).
- **Parts to find**: mostly 0.2-0.9 m (the beacon and containment pod are about 1.2-1.3 m), a clear silhouette at a distance; origin on the floor at the centre of the base, facing -Y.
- **Wall pieces** (lockers, racks, conduit, screens): back on +Y, placed flush on the ship's wall panels; 0.3-1.5 m deep, up to 2.3 m wide.
- **Interior sets**: lab (pods, jars, couch, lockers, racks, conduit, holo table), bridge (helm, nav screen, radar post), cargo (crates, drums), quarters (bunk, mess table).
- **Not here**: hulls (ships work), colour variants (paints), the MIB alien detector (not modelled in the game either; needs its own brief).
